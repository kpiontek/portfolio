## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is hiring managers and engineering leaders evaluating Kyle Piontek for senior or staff-level engineering roles. Consulting and product clients are a secondary audience. Visitors typically need to assess technical depth, product judgment, leadership, and communication quickly.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Kyle's current experience and strongest shipped work, establishes him as a Senior Full Stack Developer with more than 14 years of experience, and gives qualified visitors a clear path to review his resume or start a conversation.

Success means a visitor understands Kyle's level and range from the work itself, especially the systems he has built and led, rather than from a generic skills list.

## Positioning

The portfolio is an evidence-led account of a developer who can take ambiguous, troubled, or aging systems and turn them into stable products that work for real people. It connects enterprise and public-sector scale with recent independently built products, showing architecture, delivery, product thinking, and operational care in one body of work.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive through job applications, professional referrals, search, and direct links. They may review the site quickly on desktop or mobile, open live projects in a new tab, download the resume, or contact Kyle by email or LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a React and Vite single-page application deployed to Cloudflare Pages.
- SiteCMD is the flagship project. Visit Your Team and Was It Vibed are supporting project stories.
- The current employment record and measurable career claims come from the supplied resume.
- Project descriptions must be grounded in the project repositories and live product evidence.
- Existing working links, the headshot, and the resume remain available.
- The old dark gradient and generic developer-template visual system is not a design constraint and should be replaced.
- The finished site must remain responsive, keyboard accessible, performant, and useful without motion.

## Brand Commitments

Use Kyle Piontek's real name and a direct, technically literate voice. The tone should feel experienced, candid, calm, and specific. Avoid inflated titles, invented outcomes, generic enthusiasm, and vague claims about innovation.

The visual direction is intentionally conventional at the structural level and exceptional in execution: clean, modern, professional, and focused on the work. Avoid governing metaphors, novelty navigation, retro interfaces, scientific-instrument treatments, blueprint or drafting treatments, decorative system diagrams, and other concept-first devices. Project screenshots, typography, spacing, hierarchy, and restrained motion should carry the design.

The color system uses sage-tinted light surfaces, forest charcoal text, and deep evergreen fields. A fully opaque forest-green header anchors navigation with a shallow directional shadow. Evergreen also owns primary actions, interactive text, full-width section-title underlines, and the closing contact field; it should feel grounded and mature, never outdoorsy, rustic, or decorative. Nearly tonal dot, directional-line, and concentric-line patterns may add quiet depth at section edges. Link hover feedback is stationary: underlines disappear and text turns burnt orange, with a lighter orange on green and Deep Forest. The circular portrait is the single rounded surface and carries its own restrained photographic shadow.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with shipped systems and decisions, not a stack inventory.
2. Make seniority legible through scope, constraints, tradeoffs, and outcomes.
3. Use concrete evidence wherever a generic claim could have appeared.
4. Keep the experience personal and authored without turning it into a novelty interface.
5. Make every interaction fast, accessible, and respectful of the visitor's attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve semantic structure, keyboard navigation, visible focus, adequate contrast, reduced-motion behavior, descriptive image text, and a useful reading order at narrow widths.
