# Portfolio home

- Mode: Experience. This is a senior developer portfolio, not a product or company page.
- Audience: Senior and staff-level hiring managers first; consulting clients second.
- Job: Establish Kyle's level and judgment quickly, then make recent work and career evidence easy to inspect.
- Primary action: Review selected work. Secondary actions: download the resume, email Kyle, or visit LinkedIn.
- Proof: Brambleworks as Kyle's independent product venture; SiteCMD as the flagship case study; Visit Your Team and Was It Vibed as supporting work; 14+ years of delivery; enterprise, public-sector, and high-scale platform experience.
- Direction: Clean, modern, professional, editorial, and work-first. Sage-white surfaces, forest charcoal, a fully opaque evergreen header with a shallow directional shadow, and a Deep Forest conclusion. Green interactive text and full-width title underlines provide wayfinding; link hover stays stationary, drops the underline, and turns burnt orange. Nearly tonal dot, directional-line, and concentric-line fields add depth only at section edges. The mobile menu reveals as one solid evergreen sheet with a brief clip-path transition, stable width, body-scroll lock, focus restoration, and no opacity fade. Strong type, measured spacing, real screenshots, and restrained motion keep the work authoritative. Avoid redundant divider rules around headings and project titles. Treat the circular portrait as the only rounded surface and reserve elevation for the header and portrait.
- Memorable moment: A direct opening statement followed immediately by a large, credible SiteCMD case study with a real product image.
- Constraints: No product-marketing framing, novelty navigation, faux technical diagrams, gradient washes, glass, badge clouds, or decorative interface metaphors. Preserve the resume, headshot, working links, and factual claims.
- Unresolved: None. The user's direct direction supersedes the rejected concept sketches.
